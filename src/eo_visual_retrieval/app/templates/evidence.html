Read the metrics correctly

Cosine similarity
Proximity between normalized vectors. A retrieval score, not confidence, correctness, or a percentage.
Precision@k
Fraction of retrieved results matching the benchmark relevance rule.
mAP@k
Average precision rewards relevant results near the top; averaged over queries. This project divides each AP by min(total relevant index items, retrieved count).
nDCG@k
Rewards placing relevant results early, normalized against an ideal ranking. Here relevance is binary.
Overlap@k
Fraction of result IDs shared by two models. Agreement is not correctness.
Engine time
Time spent handling one search in the engine, including queueing. Excludes upload, decoding, model startup, and browser rendering.

Text/hybrid relevance, robustness to paraphrases, and judged usefulness are not yet benchmarked. The initial 50% hybrid weight is an adjustable default, not a tuned optimum.
